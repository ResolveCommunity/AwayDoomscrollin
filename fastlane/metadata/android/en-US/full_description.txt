AwayDoomscrollin' is an open-source, privacy-first Android app developed by Resolve Community, designed to help you break the habit of endless doomscrolling on Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts.

Features:
• Real-time Shield: Instantly detects reel/short scrolling and halts the distracting feed.
• Anti-Cheat Protection: Intervenes if you attempt to disable protection during focus mode.
• Streak Tracker: Build your daily focus streaks and stay motivated to stay away from the scroll.
• Privacy & Network Transparency: Screen-content analysis and blocking run on-device. Anonymous telemetry is enabled by default and can be disabled in the app. While enabled, it sends the device manufacturer/model, Android version and SDK level, app version, aggregate Instagram/TikTok/YouTube block counts, total blocks, streak days, and XP to awaydoomscrollin.com at startup (subject to a 24-hour throttle) and after blocking events. The app also automatically contacts GitHub when the app or accessibility service starts to fetch updated rule/configuration data; successful fetches are cached for six hours. Remote-rule fetching is separate from the telemetry switch and currently has no separate opt-out.
