Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 1, 2026

1. Introduction & Transparency Commitment

At AwayDoomscrollin', we believe digital well-being tools should disclose their behavior clearly. This policy explains accessibility processing, local storage, default-on anonymous telemetry, and automatic GitHub rule updates. We do not sell personal information or use third-party advertising analytics.

2. Network Data Collection

Screen-content analysis and blocking run on-device. Anonymous telemetry is enabled by default and can be disabled in the App. While enabled, it sends the disclosed device/app fields and aggregate blocking statistics to awaydoomscrollin.com. The App also contacts GitHub automatically for rule/configuration updates; this is independent of the telemetry switch. Screen contents, messages, passwords, and location are not transmitted.

3. Accessibility Service Permission & Scope

To detect endless scrolling behavior on target short video platforms (Instagram Reels, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts) and perform intervention actions, AwayDoomscrollin' utilizes the Android AccessibilityService API.

What Accessibility Service Does: Within supported app packages, it receives scroll, window-state, window-content, and click events and inspects accessibility node text, content descriptions, and view identifiers in memory to distinguish target feeds from safe areas.

Data handling: Accessibility content is not retained or transmitted. Screen text, messages, comments, passwords, keystrokes, contacts, photos, and browsing history are not included in network payloads.

4. Local Data Storage & Preferences

Blocking statistics, streak counters, target-app toggles, and recent shield activity are stored locally via SharedPreferences ('away_doomscroll_prefs') and are removed when the App is uninstalled. While telemetry is enabled, the disclosed aggregate block, streak, and XP fields are also submitted to awaydoomscrollin.com.

5. Third-Party Libraries & Advertisements

The App contains zero third-party advertising SDKs, zero tracking pixels, and zero commercial analytics tools. There are no sponsored ad networks integrated into AwayDoomscrollin'.

6. User Control & Permission Revocation

You retain 100% control over the App at all times. You may toggle protection shields on or off for individual apps inside the App interface, or revoke Accessibility permissions at any time via Android Settings > Accessibility > AwayDoomscrollin'.
